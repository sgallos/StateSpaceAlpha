# Reference Papers

No PDFs are committed here. This folder records the methodological anchors for
the analysis.

- Kim, Behr, Ba, Brown. State-space multitaper time-frequency analysis. PNAS,
  2018. DOI: `10.1073/pnas.1702877115`.
- Kim, Ba, Brown. A multitaper frequency-domain bootstrap method. IEEE Signal
  Processing Letters, 2018. DOI: `10.1109/LSP.2018.2876606`.
- Song, Chakravarty, Brown. A smoother state space multitaper spectrogram.
  EMBC, 2018. DOI: `10.1109/EMBC.2018.8512190`.
- Auger-Methe et al. State-space models' dirty little secrets. Scientific
  Reports, 2016. DOI: `10.1038/srep26677`.
- Auger-Methe et al. A guide to state-space modeling of ecological time
  series. Ecological Monographs, 2021. DOI: `10.1002/ecm.1470`.
- Cornelissen et al. EEG markers of brain development during sevoflurane
  anesthesia in children. British Journal of Anaesthesia, 2018.
  DOI: `10.1016/j.bja.2018.01.037`.
